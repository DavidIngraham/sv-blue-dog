Qualification conditions, rationale, open issues and verification

N-044 WetMechanicalIntegrity
External mechanisms and structural joints shall retain integrity after the selected wet-exposure campaign.
N-111 WetMechanismTravel
After each N-044 exposure, external mechanisms shall complete their full commanded travel without seizure.
N-112 WetJointIntegrity
After each N-044 exposure, structural joints shall show no separation or through-cracks on visual inspection at 5 times magnification.
