Qualification conditions, rationale, open issues and verification

C-004 SailingPropulsion
A qualifying attempt shall use sailing propulsion without auxiliary motor propulsion.
M-001 RoundTrip
The vessel shall cross the configured The Dalles departure, Bonneville turnaround and The Dalles return gates in order during one qualifying attempt.
R-004 ChallengeMotorInhibition
The vessel shall prevent motor propulsion from qualifying as unassisted sailing.
