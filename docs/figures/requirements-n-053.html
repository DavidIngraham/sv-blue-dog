N-053 SubmergedWeedPassage
The vessel shall tolerate submerged milfoil-like vegetation during the specified unassisted Gorge sailing passes.
N-126 WeedPassageSpeed
During each N-053 patch passage, mean water-relative sailing speed shall be at least 50 percent of the weed-free reference speed.
N-127 WeedPassageSteering
After each N-053 patch passage, full commanded rudder travel shall be regained within 5 minutes.
