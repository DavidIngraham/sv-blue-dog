Conditions, rationale and verification

P-003 Serviceability
The vessel shall support replacement of serviceable equipment without structural damage.
P-107 ReplacementDuration
Each P-003 replacement shall take no more than 30 minutes.
P-108 NondestructiveService
Each P-003 replacement shall leave bonded structural joints and adjacent parts intact.
P-109 PostServiceSealing
After each P-003 replacement, the assembly shall meet the N-043 immersion criterion.
P-110 PostServiceActuation
After each P-003 replacement, affected actuators shall complete their full commanded travel.
