| N-001 | EnvironmentalEnvelope | open | The vessel shall retain navigation, autonomous sail/steering control, mission recording, and available-link telemetry throughout these operating envelopes: Gorge wind 3-15 m/s with 20 m/s gusts, significant wave height up to 1.0 m with peak periods 2-5 s, and current up to 1.5 m/s; ocean wind 3-15 m/s with 20 m/s gusts, significant wave height up to 3.0 m with peak periods 5-20 s, and current up to 1.0 m/s. Wind is true wind referenced to 10 m above water, with 10-minute means and 3-second gusts; wave statistics use 20-minute records. Both profiles include air 0-40 degC, water 2-30 degC, relative humidity 0-100 percent with condensation, day/night, and visibility at least 1 km. Function shall be retained for 72 continuous hours in Gorge qualification and 30 continuous days in ocean qualification without maintenance or operator intervention, with onboard harvesting permitted. Below 3 m/s wind, the vessel shall retain navigation, recording and autonomous contingency behavior; positive progress or station keeping is not guaranteed. Current limits do not guarantee upstream progress at every wind angle. At an observed operating-limit exceedance, the vessel shall log the event and enter autonomous survival behavior within 60 s; visibility below 1 km shall trigger that behavior within 60 s of detection. Survival shall preserve flotation, attached ballast and rig, sealed electronics, mission state, and at least one time-stamped position record per minute for 24 h in wind up to 25 m/s with 35 m/s gusts, Gorge significant waves up to 2 m (peak periods 3-7 s), or ocean significant waves up to 6 m (peak periods 6-20 s). Qualification shall include individual waves up to twice the respective significant wave height, with wind/waves/current from adverse relative directions. After 10 continuous minutes back inside operating limits, autonomous sailing shall resume within 5 minutes without repair. The respective current, temperature and humidity limits also apply during survival. Survival does not require course progress and does not authorize motor use in a qualifying challenge. These are engineering design targets, not measured route extremes or demonstrated capability. |
