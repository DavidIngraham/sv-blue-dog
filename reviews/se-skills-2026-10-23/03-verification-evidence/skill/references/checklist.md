# V&V and evidence checklist

Source IDs refer to [sources.md](sources.md). For every applicable item record a finding or justified consistency/exclusion. Scope and evidence gaps remain explicit.

| ID | Review and closure question | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V01 | Is the assessed claim a requirement, stakeholder expectation or study objective? | VER5.3.1.1; VAL5.4.1.1 |
| V02 | Are applicability, subject and configuration identified? | VER preparation; MH8.19 |
| V03 | Are verification and intended-use validation distinguished? | VER/VAL process purposes |
| V04 | Are methods selected and justified for each outcome? | DSEG6.2.6; MH8.14 |
| V05 | Are criteria, conditions, units and measurement boundaries explicit before execution? | VER5.3.1.1,5.3.1.2.1 |
| V06 | Are procedure, resources, test environment and necessary users ready? | VER/VAL preparation |
| V07 | Are instruments/reference truth, time basis and calibration adequate? | VER execution; VAL execution |
| V08 | Are uncertainty and its reporting/coverage basis appropriate to the decision? | UNC7; VER results |
| V09 | Are actual observations, failures, incomplete cases and exposure retained? | VER execution/results; local completeness probe |
| V10 | Are execution result, criterion evaluation and acceptance disposition separate? | VER results/report; S2; local evidence boundary |
| V11 | Can artifact identity, source/runtime/configuration and reduction be reproduced? | MH artifact/configuration matrices; local provenance application |
| V12 | Is evidence transfer from a fixture, component, model or old configuration justified? | VER/VAL preparation/results; local applicability probe |
| V13 | Does simulation evidence remain within its verified/validated domain? | VAL5.4.1.2.3; local model-use probe |
| V14 | Are anomalies diagnosed as product, procedure, environment or model issues? | VAL5.4.1.2.2-3 |
| V15 | Do changes trigger appropriate impact review and re-assessment? | DSEG6.2.6; VAL revalidation |
| V16 | Do requirement-to-objective and evidence-to-claim traces preserve meaning? | MH8.15-8.17; S2 |
| V17 | Can missing or mismatched evidence prevent an affirmative claim? | VER/VAL criteria/results; local negative control |
| V18 | Does intended-use validation include people, recovery and interfacing products? | VAL5.4.1.2; MH8.18-8.20 |

Record native claim/case IDs, method, planned article/configuration, criterion source, procedure revision, expected data, actual artifacts, uncertainty/applicability review, evaluator/disposition, limitations and follow-up. Keep absent or undecided entries explicit; a schema row is not completed evidence.
