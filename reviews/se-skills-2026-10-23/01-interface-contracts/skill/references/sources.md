The checklist's concrete negative cases, configuration/clock identifiers and authority envelopes are local applications of the above principles. They are not quotations or universally mandated fields. Tailor them to actual hazards, protocols and existing infrastructure. INCOSE's paid handbook was not available for detailed clause inspection; no invented INCOSE clause is used to support these recommendations.
