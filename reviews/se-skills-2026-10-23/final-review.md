# Systems-engineering skills and model audit

Seven sourced skills were created, validated, installed and applied in sequence.
Each stage has its own checklist dispositions, source register, model/code review,
focused/full JUnit records and publication validation. The final model passed all 1,095 regression tests, all native cross-audits and a 36-page publication build. All seven installed skill packages and source archives passed integrity checks.

Each skill's references/sources.md and references/checklist.md identify inspected
editions/sections and the source basis of each recommendation. Primary NASA, DoD,
OMG, NIST and accessible INCOSE material inform project tailoring. Historical
public INCOSE guidance is identified; full paid handbook/ISO text was unavailable.
These artifacts do not claim full contractual standards compliance or import
agency approval boards, military thresholds, default margins or arbitrary weights.

The pinned native runtime is 23ca2e2f0f1fae4da6bc3e1488951cb017ed43e2. Model semantics,
queries, calculations and results use that runtime. The connected MCP's different
checkout/revision was excluded. No second SysML parser, relationship engine or
result ontology was introduced.

The final integrated native audits report 257 governing statements: 243 current
requirements plus two provisional current study objectives, all 245 with verification
objectives, and 12 explicitly future statements. There are 67 derivations and
166 refinements. Architecture coverage includes 14 logical responsibilities,
28 exchanges, nine scenarios with 41 steps and 85 scoped mappings. All 100
verification definitions have planned methods. New relationships carry supporting
basis/change impact; they do not automatically prove satisfaction or acceptance.

Native before/after comparison preserves all eight original disposition/evidence
statements, all six original questions/owners/evidence/acceptance flags and actual
rig development standings. All three rigs remain equal contenders. Fixed-camber
geometry remains a numerical baseline; a gearbox remains an option. Mission policy,
numerical design allocations and historical evidence are preserved. Original-revision
verification passed 2,262 archived source fingerprints across 23 archives and
101 manifest-registered files. The archive publisher's geometry-change guard remains
intact, with all four original geometry-source digests matching.

## Open engineering work

The audit makes the remaining evidence needs explicit rather than closing them
with model structure or supplied Booleans. U01-U06 remain open: installed traffic
criteria/rule applicability, course-wide recovery and retrieval, structural
qualification, installed sensing/achieved control, integrated acceptance coverage,
and separately scoped future missions.

Interface clock/frame/freshness/arbitration and implementation choices still need
engineering evidence. Safety-control effectiveness, residual-risk disposition,
rate/dependence/common-cause evidence and complete lifecycle coverage remain open.
Actual article, hardware/software/data, procedure and instrument identities are
missing where declared. Current whole-vessel mass and power peaks require identified
coupled/installed evidence. Analysis validation, uncertainty and domain adequacy are
use-specific; 110 use annotations are not 110 physical validation claims. Rig choice
requires comparable installation/performance/energy/durability/casualty and relevant
construction/cost/schedule evidence. No synthetic production scores or approvals
were invented to fill those gaps.

Each completed stage preserves source fingerprints and a source ZIP with an
integrity manifest. Each manifest declares its scope; later stages also include skill drafts. Installed skills are checked separately. These snapshots identify their recorded source files;
they are not a complete execution environment or accepted physical evidence bundle.
See validation-summary.json in each stage and deliverable-integrity.json for checks.
