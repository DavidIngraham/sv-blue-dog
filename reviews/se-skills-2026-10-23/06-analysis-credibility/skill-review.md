# Skill review and realistic probes

Validated entrypoint/frontmatter and source links. Four installed files match their
workspace drafts. Review was performed directly under AGENTS.md (no delegation).

- Request an archived design as a missing current result: reject; producer named.
- Request CAD/polar as explicit reference: preserve manifest validation and original
  provenance; do not compare old drivers to today's source tree.
- Good source hash, bad driver hash: both record/path consumers reject.
- Missing all fingerprints: workspace result is unproven, not implicitly accepted.
- Use an old fixed design as a starting guess: permitted only under explicit seed
  role; all dimensions remain free and are reevaluated in the current model.
- Add an unclassified native analysis: discovery finds it, register audit fails.
- Review a valid analysis that finds the design infeasible: do not turn that into
  a failed calculation or require design success for credibility eligibility.
- Claim physical validation from110 metadata rows or a regression run: report the
  missing measurements/domain/uncertainty evidence; no credibility score invented.

Skill recommendations are mapped to inspected primary sources. NASA agency
applicability and the unavailable full paid standards are explicitly distinguished.
