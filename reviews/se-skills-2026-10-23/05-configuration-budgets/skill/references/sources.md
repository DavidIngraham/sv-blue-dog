# Primary sources and tailoring

Checked 2026-10-23. Recommendations are project applications, not certification.

| ID | Inspected edition and locator | Use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CM | [NASA SE Handbook §6.5](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-5-configuration-management/), §§6.5.1.2.1–6.5.1.2.4 and configuration-audit/work-product subsections | Identification, change/status accounting and verification. The web page misnumbers its last two subsections as 6.4.1.2.5 and 6.4.1.2.6. Do not import NASA review gates or a CCB into another project's workflow. |
| TA | [NASA SE Handbook §6.7](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-7-technical-assessment/), §6.7.1.2 | Defined measures, consistent reporting and feedback. |
| TM | [PSM/INCOSE/industry Technical Measurement, INCOSE-TP-2003-020-01, v1.0, 27 December 2005](https://www.incose.org/docs/default-source/ProductsPublications/technical-measurement-guide---dec-2005.pdf?sfvrsn=4&sfvrsn=4), §§3.2.5, 6.3.3–6.3.6 and 6.4 | Historical public guidance by Garry Roedler and Cheryl Jones: risk-relevant measures, explicit aggregation, collection basis and responsibility. Survey counts and old referenced editions are not current mandates. |
| DSE | [Defense SE Guidebook, September 2026](https://www.cto.mil/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SE-Guidebook-2026.pdf), §6.1.6 (pp.117–120) and §6.1.3 (pp.110–112) | Configuration consistency and change impact; estimate, contingency, threshold and margin. Illustrated contingency percentages are examples, not adopted allocations. Cover/approval identify 2026 despite stale PDF title metadata. |
| SI | [BIPM SI Brochure, 9th ed., v4.01, June 2026](https://www.bipm.org/documents/d/guest/si-brochure-9-en-pdf), chapters 2 and 5 | Quantities, units and reported values; source values must retain their accounting meaning. |
| S2 | [OMG SysML 2.0 Part 1, formal/2026-03-02](https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML/2.0/Language/PDF), §§7.6.2–7.6.7, 7.11, 7.13 and 7.19–7.20 | Definitions/usages, variants, composition, bindings and calculations. Tool support must be checked at the pinned revision. |

Full paid EIA-649, ISO and INCOSE handbook texts were not inspected. The defense
guidebook's references to them are not a substitute for reading their clauses.
Concrete manifests, audit rules and regression controls are tailored applications;
no universal margin, approval authority or required budget number is asserted.
