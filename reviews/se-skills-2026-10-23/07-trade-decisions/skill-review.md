five installed files. Native example uses synthetic values solely to exercise the

failed/unknown provisional reliability target does not become mandatory veto;

error/unavailable evaluation; admitted-subset membership is supplied, not inferred
from a high score; numerical baseline is distinct from hardware choice; no actual
authority/date or acceptance is invented for an open question. Reopening triggers
link future evidence/configuration changes to the actual question being reconsidered.
