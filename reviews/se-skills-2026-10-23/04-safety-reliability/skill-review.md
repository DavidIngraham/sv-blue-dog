# Safety/reliability skill decision probes

Manual review; no independent agent or physical acceptance.

| Probe | Expected response | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treat a populated FMEA as a complete safety case. | Require consequence-first scenarios, modes, controls and evidence; leave completeness open. | Six initial scenarios and scopeReviewed=false. |
| Credit separate recovery equipment as independent. | Review shared supply, sensing, configuration, clock and software mechanisms. | FM015 and common-cause/dependence review preconditions. |
| Close a hazard by setting Boolean flags. | Require actual evidence/disposition identities and authority; flags remain assertions. | Native closure negative controls for every record and review field. |
| Add component rates without establishing mission-critical boundaries. | Require non-overlap, applicable failure definition and dependence treatment. | Three explicit rate-model review preconditions; no rates changed. |
| Adopt the military risk matrix or an arbitrary RPN cutoff. | Tailor to actual project authority and criteria; missing assessment remains unassessed. | No matrix, score, probability or authority invented. |
| Require radio outage to abort or require harvesting on a finite passage. | Read current authoritative policy and applicability. | Original conditional noncritical rows and mission rules preserved. |
| Mark controls effective because software tests pass. | Separate implementation/effectiveness evidence from native regression. | All six dispositions and thirteen critical rows remain open. |

Source basis: skill references/sources.md and checklist S01–S18. Unknown physical coverage, risk criteria and accepted records remain explicit closure work.
