Concrete model flags, hazard-record fields, mutation controls and case mappings are tailored applications of these principles, not mandated field names. Full paid INCOSE, ISO, IEC and SAE texts were not inspected; no unseen clause or universal score/rate is asserted. Preserve the distinction between control intent, actual evidence and the responsible person's risk disposition.
